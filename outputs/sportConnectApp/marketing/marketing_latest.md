# SportConnect Marketing Kit

## One-Line Pitch
Find your next pickup game in seconds — no app download required.

## Elevator Pitch
SportConnect is a real-time web platform that connects local athletes with pickup sports games happening near them. Whether you're organizing a Sunday football match or looking to join a basketball game tonight, SportConnect puts everything on an interactive map — browse, join, and get live updates instantly, right in your browser. No app store, no friction, just three clicks to game time.

---

## Target Audience & Positioning

**Primary Audience:**
- **Casual/Amateur Athletes (18–40)** in urban and suburban areas who play informally (football, basketball, tennis, etc.) and struggle to find consistent people to play with.
- **Community Organizers** — the "regulars" who already run weekly games via group chats and want a simpler way to manage rosters and attract new players.
- **Spontaneous Players** who decide last-minute they want to play and need to find a game *right now*, nearby.

**Positioning Statement:**
For casual athletes who want to play more and coordinate less, SportConnect is a web-based pickup sports platform that makes finding and joining local games as easy as checking a map — unlike group chats, spreadsheets, or mobile-only apps, SportConnect requires no download, no signup friction, and delivers real-time updates the moment plans change.

**Key Differentiators:**
- Zero install — works instantly in any browser
- Real-time roster & cancellation updates (no more "wait, is this still happening?")
- Map-first discovery — see games near you at a glance
- Fast: under 2-second load, 3 clicks to join
